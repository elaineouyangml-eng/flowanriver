Letters to the Future

You may forget, but time will remember.

Imagine opening your inbox one ordinary day and finding an unexpected letter. The sender is no one else but you, writing from a few months or even years ago.

The letter might remind you of how you felt back then, what life was like, or the people and things that once meant so much to you. Perhaps you see things differently now. Perhaps those worries are long gone, or you've almost forgotten why you wrote those words in the first place.

You may have forgotten, but time has kept that moment for you.

So, what would you like to leave for your future self?

You don't need a special reason, and you don't have to wait for an important occasion. Write whatever is on your mind today, choose a date in the future, and let these words find their way back to you.

想象一下，也许在未来的某一天， 你像往常一样打开邮箱，却意外收到一封信。

寄件人不是别人，而是几个月前，或是几年前的自己。

信里写着那时的心情、正在经历的生活， 还有一些曾经很在意的人和事。 也许现在的你已经有了不同的答案， 也许那些烦恼早已过去， 甚至连当初为什么写下这些话都记不清了。

你可能已经忘记，但时间替你记住了那一刻。

如果可以，你想给未来的自己留下什么？

不需要特别的理由，也不必等到重要的日子。 写下此刻想说的话，选一个未来的日期， 让它在某一天重新回到你身边。
