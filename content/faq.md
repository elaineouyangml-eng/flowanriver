# Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Flowanriver?

Flowanriver started as an independent blog about AI, crypto, and the little things that make us curious about the world.

Over time, it has begun to grow beyond just writing. We're exploring new ways to connect ideas, technology, and everyday life, including a feature that lets you send a letter to your future self.

I don't know exactly what Flowanriver will look like a few years from now, and that's part of what makes building it interesting.

### Who is behind Flowanriver?

Flowanriver is an independent project created and developed by Elaine.

The stories here come from my own experiences, questions, observations, and things I've learned along the way. Sometimes I'm sharing something I understand well. Other times, I'm still figuring it out as I write.

I think there's room for both.

### Are your articles written by AI?

AI is part of my working process, but it isn't a replacement for my own thinking.

I use AI tools to help organize research, refine language, translate articles, and occasionally explore different ways of explaining an idea.

But the topics I choose, the experiences I share, the questions I ask, and the final decisions about what gets published come from me.

I'm not interested in pretending AI doesn't exist. I'm more interested in learning how to use it without losing my own voice.

### What topics do you write about?

Flowanriver has three main sections:

**AI** — Tools, new developments, practical experiences, and questions about how artificial intelligence is changing our lives and work.

**Crypto** — Blockchain, digital assets, stablecoins, Web3, regulation, security, and the gradual development of real-world applications.

**Journal** — Personal reflections on life, culture, human nature, growth, and the moments that stay with us.

I don't try to cover every headline. I write about the things that make me want to understand a little more.

### Is Flowanriver part of Pi Network?

Flowanriver has been exploring the Pi Network ecosystem, including opportunities to develop and test applications within it.

That doesn't mean Flowanriver is an official Pi Network platform or represents the Pi Core Team.

I'm interested in how ecosystems develop, how people actually use new technologies, and what happens between an idea and its real-world application.

I also believe it's important to keep asking questions, even when we're excited about something.

### What are Future Letters?

Future Letters is a feature that lets you write a message to your future self.

You write a letter, choose a future date, and provide your email address. After confirming your email, your letter can be scheduled to return to you on the date you've chosen.

Maybe it's something you want to remember, a promise you've made, or just a few words from the person you are today.

You can explore it here:

[Write a Letter to Your Future Self](https://flowanriver.com/letters/)

### Are my Future Letters private?

Your letters are intended to be personal. They aren't published as articles or displayed publicly on Flowanriver.

The service processes information needed to confirm your email, schedule the letter, and attempt delivery.

As with any online service, there are technical and operational risks, so please avoid including passwords, private keys, recovery phrases, or highly sensitive personal information.

You can read more in our [Privacy Policy](https://flowanriver.com/privacy-policy/).

### Can I edit or cancel a letter after sending it?

Future Letters is designed around writing and sealing a message for a future date.

Editing or cancellation options may not be available once a letter has been confirmed. Please review your message, email address, and delivery date carefully before submitting it.

If you experience a problem, you can contact us at [contact@flowanriver.com](mailto:contact@flowanriver.com).

### Why are some articles still in English when I choose another language?

Flowanriver currently supports six languages:

English, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Vietnamese, and Filipino.

Not every article has been translated into every language.

When a translation is available, the website aims to show that version first. When it isn't, you'll see the original English article instead.

You can also use your browser's translation feature, such as Google Translate in Chrome, to read an English article in your preferred language.

### How do you handle translations?

I want readers from different places to be able to explore the ideas shared here.

AI tools may help with translation, but I also review and adjust the content to preserve the original meaning, context, and tone.

Translations can never capture every nuance perfectly, especially when an article involves technical concepts or personal observations.

If there is a difference in interpretation, please refer to the English original.

### Is the Crypto content financial advice?

No.

The Crypto section is a place for learning, research, observation, and discussion.

Articles should not be treated as personalized investment, trading, legal, or financial advice.

Digital assets involve risks, and readers should independently verify information before making decisions.

### Will Flowanriver have a store or other features in the future?

Possibly.

I've thought about many directions Flowanriver could take — an online store, creative tools, new digital experiences, or something we haven't even imagined yet.

I'm also interested in exploring how emerging technologies, including Web3, might shape what an independent platform can become.

But these are ideas, not promises.

For now, I'd rather build things carefully, learn from what works, and leave space for the unexpected.

### How often do you publish?

There's no fixed schedule.

Sometimes a new technology makes me curious. Sometimes a conversation, a book, or an ordinary day gives me something to think about.

I publish when I have something I want to explore or share.

### Can I republish or translate your articles?

You're welcome to share links to Flowanriver articles.

If you'd like to republish a complete article, create a substantial translation for another website, or use the content commercially, please contact me first.

Original content should not be republished without permission, except where permitted by applicable law.
