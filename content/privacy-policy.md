## 3. Future Letters

Future Letters is a feature designed to let you write a message to your future self and receive it on a date you choose.

To operate this service, Flowanriver may process:

- Your email address.
- The content of your letter.
- Your selected delivery date.
- Email verification status.
- Scheduling and delivery information.
- Technical information necessary to operate and protect the service.

### Email Verification

Before a letter is scheduled for delivery, we require confirmation of the email address provided.

This helps reduce unauthorized submissions and improves the reliability of the service.

### Privacy of Your Letters

Future Letters is intended to be a personal experience.

Your letters are not published as articles or made publicly accessible through Flowanriver.

We do not use your private letters as public website content or for advertising copy without your permission.

Information may be processed by the systems and service providers necessary to store, schedule, and deliver the letters.

Technical access may also be required in limited circumstances involving service operation, security, legal obligations, or incident investigation.

### Security and Storage

We aim to use appropriate safeguards to protect letter information during storage, transmission, and delivery.

The specific technical protections depend on the service infrastructure and its implemented security controls.

No online system can guarantee absolute security.

We encourage you not to include passwords, wallet recovery phrases, private keys, financial account credentials, or other highly sensitive information in your letters.

### Delivery

Future Letters is designed to deliver your message on the selected date after successful verification and scheduling.

Actual delivery may be affected by email providers, network interruptions, operational issues, or other circumstances beyond our control.

We cannot guarantee delivery at an exact time or guarantee that every email will reach its intended inbox.

### Retention and Deletion

Letter information is retained as necessary for verification, scheduling, delivery, security, and applicable legal obligations.

Retention may differ depending on whether a letter is unverified, scheduled, delivered, or affected by a delivery failure.

We will maintain retention and deletion practices consistent with the service's actual technical arrangements and applicable law.

If you have questions about the handling or deletion of your information, contact us at contact@flowanriver.com.
